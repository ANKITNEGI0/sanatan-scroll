Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

ScrollVerse is a verse by verse reading app for the Bhagavad Gita, the Ramayana, the Mahabharata and the Hanuman Chalisa. This policy explains what information we collect when you use ScrollVerse, why we collect it, and what we do with it. By using ScrollVerse you agree to this policy.

Signing in with Google

ScrollVerse offers optional sign in with Google. If you choose to sign in, Google shares your name, your email address and your profile picture with us, so we can identify your account and show your name and picture inside the app.

Signing in is optional. You can read every verse without an account. We never see or store your Google password, and we do not ask Google for access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google service. We only receive the basic profile information listed above.

What we save

To make the app useful across visits and devices, we save your reading activity and preferences on our servers, linked to your account when you are signed in:

  • Your reading streak, including the days you read and how many verses you have read in total and today.
  • The verses you save with the bookmark button, and the verses you like.
  • Your reading position in each text, so you can resume where you left off.
  • Your progress in reading plans, including which plan days you have completed.
  • Your settings, such as language (English or Hindi), verse text size and your reading goal.
  • Your ScrollVerse Pro status, including which plan you are on and when it renews or ends.

How we use this information

We use the information above only to run ScrollVerse for you: to show your streak, your saved verses and your progress, to resume your reading where you left off, and to unlock Pro features on your account. We do not use it to build advertising profiles, and ScrollVerse carries no third party advertising trackers.

We do not share your information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing or any other purpose. The only services involved are the ones needed to run the app itself: Google, for sign in, and our hosting and database providers, which store the data on our behalf. If the law ever requires us to disclose information, we will share only what the law requires.

Data stored on your device

ScrollVerse stores a few small items on your own device, such as your light or dark theme choice and your sign in session, so the app opens the way you left it. These stay on your device and are not sent to us, apart from the reading data listed above.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your reading data while your account is active, so your streak, saves and Pro status are there when you return. If you would like your data deleted, or a copy of what we hold about you, write to us at ankitnegi.works@gmail.com and we will delete or share it as requested.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in an important way, we will update this page and note the new date at the top. Continuing to use ScrollVerse after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact us

Questions about privacy or your data: write to us at ankitnegi.works@gmail.com.
